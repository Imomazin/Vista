# VisTa capability register

Maintained under the *Enterprise Intelligence and Activation* brief, as revised by the owner's *Proprietary Intelligence Infrastructure and External Cost Independence* direction (28 November 2026): VisTa builds and owns its intelligence infrastructure ([architecture](proprietary-intelligence.md)), paid intelligence subscriptions are not purchased and are never prerequisites, and existing provider adapters stay optional. Each capability records its status, the evidence behind that status, and the dependency that blocks the next step. A configured key does not prove a working API, and a deployed page does not prove a working feature.

**Status values:** *Live-tested* (exercised against the real provider or site in production, with evidence) · *Deployed* (in production, not yet live-tested end to end) · *Fixture-tested* (verified against fixtures or simulated providers only) · *Implemented* (code exists, not verified) · *Blocked* (needs an owner or provider action) · *Not started*.

Last updated: 28 November 2026 (production `vista.ambidexters.com`).
