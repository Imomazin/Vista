# VisTa product roadmap

## Foundation
- Responsive intelligence dashboard, five pillars and nine-engine catalogue
- Public HTTPS website audit and signal-level recommendations
- Automated build checks and secure development defaults

## First-party intelligence
- Identity and multi-tenant access controls
- Consent-based integrations for Google Search Console and GA4
- Search impressions, traffic, engagement and performance analytics
- Data provenance and customer-controlled retention

## AI exposure
- Provider-compliant sampling and transparent measurement methods
- Time-stamped prompts, citations, recommendations, competitor comparisons
- Country and repeated-sample variation without unsupported guarantees

## Commercial operations
- Content studio and editorial approvals
- Entity authority mapping and digital PR opportunity identification
- White-label agency dashboards and client permissions
- Conversion intelligence, CRM, subscriptions and billing

## Gates before public marketing
- Trademark review and brand clearance
- Authentication, persistent quotas and rate limits
- Security, accessibility and privacy testing
- Pilot benchmarks and external customer validation
